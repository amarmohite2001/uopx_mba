# MGT-526 Week 4: Topic 7 Quiz Answers (Teams)

| # | Answer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A. Norms focus on "how" to work together, while charters include "what" and "why" elements. | A charter covers purpose, goals, scope, roles, and processes; norms are the behavioral "how." |
| 2 | D. Each person's success depends on others' success, creating motivation for collaboration. | Interdependence means outcomes are shared and linked, not just tasks compiled or people being interchangeable. |
| 3 | B. Creatively solving complex issues quickly and with much detail. | Transcription, calendar analysis, and charter templates are named AI uses; creative problem solving is a human strength. |
| 4 | A. The team's mission statement and SMART goals. | Purpose and goals are the "what" and "why" (charter). Disagreement handling, meeting expectations, and decision processes are norms. |
| 5 | D. Building trust and relationships is more difficult through virtual channels. | The core virtual-team challenge is trust and connection, not skill, expertise access, or unreliable technology. |
| 6 | B. Members have different communication styles, priorities, and functional cultures. | Cross-functional teams bring together different departmental cultures and priorities, which raises coordination cost. |
| 7 | B. A project team that is also cross-functional. | Temporary, with a defined deliverable and end date (project), and specialists from different functions (cross-functional); co-located, so not virtual. |
| 8 | C. Low psychological safety within the team. | Interrupting and members who stop contributing ideas are classic signs people do not feel safe speaking up. |
| 9 | A. Establish different decision-making approaches for different types of decisions. | Best practice is to match the method (consensus, leader decides, vote) to the decision's importance and urgency. |
| 10 | C. Teams operating independently with visible performance metrics. | In the Whole Foods example, self-directed teams are held accountable through transparent, visible results. |

**Answer key:** 1-A, 2-D, 3-B, 4-A, 5-D, 6-B, 7-B, 8-C, 9-A, 10-C
